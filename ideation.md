An offline-first messaging app for nearby people

Imagine students in a classroom, people at an event, or a group travelling together.

They want to communicate even when mobile data is unavailable.

You could investigate an app that supports:

Nearby text messaging.
Local group conversations.
Sharing small files.
Sending messages over supported local connections.
Storing messages on the device.
Synchronizing or forwarding messages when internet access returns.

Android supports technologies such as Bluetooth and Wi-Fi-based communication, but that does not automatically give you a reliable, unlimited-range messaging network.

The limitation: Without an internet connection or a suitable communication network, you cannot guarantee delivery to someone far away.
